ReviewTrail methodology v1.0 — 2026-10-24

Reviews are subjective, self-reported accounts, not benchmark measurements. Every aggregate shows its sample size. Cells with fewer than three reviews are suppressed. Possible-shift notices require at least five reviews in each consecutive week and a mean outcome drop of at least 1.0; they do not confirm regressions.

Version, task, cost, retry and outcome fields are supplied by reviewers. Proof badges mean only that a self-attested usage line was supplied and hashed; ReviewTrail does not verify it.
